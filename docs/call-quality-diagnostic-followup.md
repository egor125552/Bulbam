# Diagnostic follow-up: call quality switching, 24 Oct 2026

Review of the supplied on-device log (900 most recent entries, ~19:01:32 to 19:06:35Z): four user-initiated profile switches (music, normal, clean, studio) each ended with `ошибка переключения`. The old logger omitted DOMException type, so there is no definitive explanation for rejection. A plausible failure is a second `getUserMedia()` while another live track already owns a phone microphone, or `RTCRtpSender.replaceTrack()` rejecting incompatible channel configurations. Both failure modes now have a safe fallback to the existing track. Full constraints are attempted first, then just echoCancellation/noiseSuppression/autoGainControl if the device rejects mid-call channel/sample-rate changes. The old audio stream is retained until a new one is working.

The new log records an allowlisted error.name (e.g. NotReadableError, InvalidModificationError, OverconstrainedError) without its potentially identifying message; it clearly distinguishes full replacement, partial application, and failure. Successful periodic GET call-state/signal responses are aggregated (every 10 with a count), while failures and mutating operations retain separate entries. This prevents 3 log lines per poll from displacing the beginning of a call.

The second call in the supplied log started at 19:02:54.293Z, was accepted at 19:02:58.015Z, and reached audio connected at 19:02:58.965Z: about 4.7 seconds from click, 0.95 seconds from acceptance. The direct ICE route was established, with relay ICE also discovered. The log has two unhandled Promise rejection notifications but no exception name or error text; their root cause cannot be recovered from this export. HTTP polling mostly returned 200 with low latency, so the profile-switch failure does not correlate with a server error.

No browser-only patch can guarantee bypassing Android/iOS audio HAL effects or preserve genuine stereo when the WebRTC codec/driver cannot switch channel count. A real on-device comparison after deployment is required; do not claim the bug is resolved on all phones based solely on unit tests.
